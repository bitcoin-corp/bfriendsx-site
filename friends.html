bFriendsX

Bring your friends.

Open the data export from each network you use. You pick who to bring, and each person gets their own link. When they join bWalletX their badge turns into a gold b.

1. Open your exports

  • f Facebook
    Settings → Download your information → Connections → Friends, format JSON.
  • ig Instagram
    Accounts Centre → Download your information → Followers and following, format JSON.
  • in LinkedIn
    Settings → Data privacy → Get a copy of your data → Connections.
  • X X
    Settings → Your account → Download an archive. X gives account numbers only, so names show later.

Want friends to find you? Link your X handle to your bWalletX.
